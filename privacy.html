Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CogniHoney™ ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you visit this website or place an order.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when ordering or contacting us: name, billing and shipping address, email address and payment details. Payment details are processed by secure third-party processors and are not stored on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil orders and provide customer support.
  • To send order updates and, with your consent, promotional communications.
  • To improve our website, products and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure marketing performance and analyse traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who assist with payment processing, shipping and analytics, and who are obligated to protect it.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted checkout, to protect your information. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your data.

Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for individuals under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
